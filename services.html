What we do

3BC designs, builds and runs the systems that let a small company operate with AI officers under human control. This is what we do today.

  • Genesis AI OS – a governed operating system for an AI-run company One official record of members, titles, decisions and policies; role cards that define each officer's duties and limits; routing of questions to the right officer; budgets and spend caps per officer; pause and stop switches; and an approval chain in which officers review and recommend and the Founder alone approves.
  • The Founder Desk A web and phone workplace where the Founder reads briefings, talks to officers in rooms, sees status, approves pull requests and holds the only keys to money, secrets and appointments. Sign-in is PIN-based from any device.
  • Officer messaging, email, SMS and voice Officers answer on the Desk and, within written rules approved by the Founder, reply to email and text messages through one shared company address. The Founder can call the officers live; nothing is recorded except a text transcript.
  • Committees and an advisory board Finance & Procurement, People & Appointments, Legal & Risk, Records & Governance, Dev Group, Dev Ops and All hands run on a schedule with written mandates, minutes and a decision log. An AI advisory board recommends everything to the Founder and decides nothing.
  • Application building Engineering lanes build and maintain the platform – the Desk, the phone app, the integrations – with independent code review, test builds and signed checks before any merge. New applications are built from scratch in the same way.
  • Independent assurance Independent QA, an internal auditor and a separate quality-and-assurance seat check the work: every claim needs evidence, and no one approves their own work.

What we do not do

  • Our officers never pay or move money, book or buy on anyone's behalf, hold customer funds, or sign anything.
  • Our officers do not give legal, financial or investment advice in a professional capacity; their output is for the Founder's decision.
  • We hold no card or bank details; payment providers handle those.

The officers are 3BC AI officers. They give advice only; decisions are the Founder's.
