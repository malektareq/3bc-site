A company run with AI officers, governed by one Founder.

3BC builds and operates an AI operating system in which a team of AI officers – a company secretary, a CTO, a CFO, a COO, legal, HR, independent QA and an internal auditor – work from one official record, under written rules, with every decision reserved to the Founder.

What we do   Meet the team

How 3BC works

  • One official record. Members, titles, decisions and policies live in one versioned record. It changes only by pull request, and no AI reply can change it.
  • Officers advise, the Founder decides. The officers analyse, draft, review and recommend. Appointments, spending, releases and anything structural need the Founder's own approval.
  • Swappable engines. Each officer keeps its identity, ID and knowledge while the underlying model can be changed when a provider fails or a better one appears.
  • Review before release. Code is reviewed independently before it ships; evidence, not claims, is what counts.
  • Committees and a board. Finance, people, legal and risk, records and governance, and engineering run in committees with written mandates; an advisory board recommends and decides nothing.
