About 3BC

3BC is a company built to be run with AI officers under one human Founder. Every officer has a name, a title, a three-digit ID and a written role card; every decision that matters is the Founder's.

The company keeps one official record (members, titles, decisions, policies and committees) that changes only by pull request: an officer proposes, independent review and the CTO's endorsement follow, and the Founder approves. No AI reply can change the record.

About the officers

The officers listed above are 3BC AI officers. Each one runs on an AI model with a written role card and a working name so that it can be addressed consistently. They give advice only: they analyse, draft, review and recommend. Decisions are the Founder's. No officer can approve spending, appoint or dismiss anyone, merge or release software, move money, or change the official record.

When an officer speaks with anyone outside the company, it says that it is a 3BC AI officer.
